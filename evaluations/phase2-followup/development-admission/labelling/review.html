Occurrence review

How to judge (identity contract)

SAME OCCURRENCE: the reports concern the same underlying occurrence, action or change, despite language, outlet, framing, detail updates or supporting documents. DIFFERENT OCCURRENCE: they concern distinct occurrences or actions, even within the same crisis, visit, summit, disaster, person or storyline (for example a warning vs the evacuation, a meeting vs a later agreement, an attack vs the retaliation). AMBIGUOUS: only when the evidence shown is insufficient to decide, not because the story is complex.

Keys: s same · d different · a ambiguous · ←/→ move · u next unlabelled. Each verdict is saved immediately.
