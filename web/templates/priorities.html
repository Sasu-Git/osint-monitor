How priorities work

HIGH
Listed first and emphasised on the map.
NORMAL
The default. The pipeline's ranking order applies.
LOW
Listed after NORMAL and drawn quieter.
MUTED
Hidden from the feed and the map until "Show muted" is ticked.

A development concerns a country when it is located there or when its principal actors act for it (a US–China meeting in Geneva concerns Switzerland as its location and the US and China as actors). A muted topic hides a development; a muted country hides it only when every country it concerns is muted.

Priorities are presentation only. They do not change which developments exist, which Situation a development belongs to, its confidence or its evidence.
